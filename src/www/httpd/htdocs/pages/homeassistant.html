1. Easiest: ONVIF integration

Home Assistant → Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → ONVIF. The camera is usually found automatically, otherwise enter:


            
You get the camera with live stream and snapshots. Motion events come via MQTT (step 3).

        


        
2. Fast live view: go2rtc (built into Home Assistant)

        

            
Add to configuration.yaml, restart, then add a Generic camera with the stream rtsp://127.0.0.1:8554/NAME or use the WebRTC card. Main stream for viewing, substream for dashboards and detection.

            

        


        
3. Motion, person detection and switches: MQTT

        

            
Enable MQTT with your broker (e.g. the Mosquitto add-on) under MQTT, then enable Home Assistant discovery under MQTT advertise. The camera then appears as a device with motion, person detection, recording switches, Wi-Fi signal, SD space and more – no YAML needed.

            

        


        
4. Availability sensor (optional)

        

            
A binary sensor that turns off when the camera hangs (health check: camera process and RTSP server).
